3. Choosing and deleting

A swipe puts a file in the queue rather than deleting it right away. Until you confirm, you can review the queue and change your mind.

Swipy does not delete files without your confirmation. Moving to Trash, deleting and emptying Trash are carried out only through an Android system request: a file is deleted only if you confirm that request. On some devices the request lets you look at the files being deleted once more.

Swipy does not work with files in the background while the app is closed. Queued compression runs while the app is open and creates new files without changing the originals. Local reminders may arrive while the app is closed, but they do not access files.

By default, Swipy moves files to the system Trash. You can choose permanent deletion in the settings. Recovery from Trash is governed by the system; Swipy does not recover permanently deleted files. Deletion may spread to your cloud gallery and other devices through sync that you have turned on.

Before confirming, check the files and back up important materials. Statistics count deletions confirmed by the system; storage may actually be freed later, for example after Trash is emptied.

4. Compression

Compression creates a separate copy and may change the photo's quality, format and metadata. Space savings depend on the original file and the chosen settings; the same result is not guaranteed for every photo. The original is not overwritten; it joins the deletion queue only after the new copy has been verified as saved.

The automatic check of the saved copy does not replace your own assessment of its quality. Compare the result with the original before confirming deletion. Compression does not guarantee that personal information is removed from metadata.
